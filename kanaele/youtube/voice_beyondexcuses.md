# voice.md – BeyondExcuses
(Für die YouTube-Skills: vor der Nutzung nach `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md` kopieren.)

## Who I am talking to
One person (16–30) who knows exactly what they should be doing and still doesn't – scrolls at night, plans instead of starting, waits for motivation. They want a 15-second mirror, not a lecture.

## How I actually talk
No narrator. Two faceless stick figures act it out; short bubbles and a three-line ending carry the message. Calm, direct, a little bit uncomfortable:
"Did you scroll all night?" · "Same night. Same hours. One of them built stairs." · "Plant. Wait. Trust." · "Let it go. Swim."

## Words I never use
hustle, grind, alpha, sigma, millionaire mindset, "you won't believe", "subscribe", game-changer, insane.

## Words I do use
you / your, still, every day, same … same …, short imperative endings ("Start anyway.", "Step out.").

## What I will not claim
No statistics or study results unless sourced. No promises of money or success. No medical or therapy advice. Never mock the grey character – it is the viewer on a bad day.

## My format
15–18 s vertical Shorts, white mannequin figures (amber = positive, grey = negative), one location and light per episode, own piano/pad music, hook at 0 s, turn at ~12 s, three-line ending above the caption area, "Follow for part N+1". Daily upload, numbered as a series.
