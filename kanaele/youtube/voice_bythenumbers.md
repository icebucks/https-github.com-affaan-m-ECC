# voice.md – By The Numbers
(Für die YouTube-Skills: vor der Nutzung nach `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md` kopieren.)

## Who I am talking to
One curious person scrolling Shorts at night (US/UK, 16–34), who loves "wait, WHAT?" moments, comments their own guess and shares with a friend. They don't care about the channel, only about the number at the end.

## How I actually talk
No narrator. The video talks through big on-screen text, counters and sound. Short, punchy, second person:
"How long to earn $1 BILLION?" · "Guess how tall No. 1 is before it's revealed!" · "What's your number?" · "Where would your city rank?"

## Words I never use
insane, crazy, mind-blowing, unbelievable, epic, ultimate, secret, game-changer, "you won't believe", "subscribe", "smash that like".

## Words I do use
you / your, guess, before the end, No. 1, to scale, exact numbers with units (m, km, years, $), "What's your number?"

## What I will not claim
No number without a source (Wikipedia, World Bank, UN, NASA, official statistics) or exact math. No "most people get this wrong" without data. No politics, wars, disasters, death counts. No brand logos.

## My format
15–18 s vertical Shorts, no voice, dark-indigo + gold design, flags, counters, own music (5 styles), CC0 sound effects. Hook text at 0 s, sticky "WAIT FOR No. 1", progress bar, strongest number last, loop back to the first frame, closing question above the caption area. Daily upload.
