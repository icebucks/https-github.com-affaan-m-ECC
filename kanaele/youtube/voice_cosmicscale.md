# voice.md – Cosmic Scale (long-form, narrated)
(Für die YouTube-Skills: vor der Nutzung nach `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md` kopieren.)

## Who I am talking to
One curious person (18–40, US/UK) who watches space videos at night, loves the feeling of feeling small, and has asked "but how long would that actually take?" They are smart but not a physicist.

## How I actually talk
Calm, precise, second person, short sentences, one number at a time, then a comparison a human can feel:
"It's forty trillion kilometers." · "You'd have had to start walking before the first animal had eyes." · "Proposed – not built."
Narrator voice: Kokoro TTS (chosen voice in KANAL.md), speed 0.95, numbers written out in words in the script.

## Words I never use
insane, mind-blowing, crazy, terrifying (unless quoting), "you won't believe", "smash that like", "before we start", channel intros, sponsor-style hype.

## Words I do use
you / your, "for scale", "here's the catch", "proposed – not built", exact units (km, km/s, light-years), "about" when rounding.

## What I will not claim
No number without a source (NASA, ESA, JPL, peer-reviewed or Wikipedia with citation) and its date. Proposals are called proposals. Rounding is said out loud ("about"). No aliens-as-fact, no conspiracy, no speculation dressed as science.

## My format
8–12 minutes, narrated, no face. Hook in the first 12 seconds, the turn by 0:35, 5–8 beats with on-screen scale animations (By-The-Numbers engine: scale zooms, race lanes, counters, timelines), payoff that answers the hook explicitly, ONE ask at the end. Chapters on every video. One long video per week + 3 Shorts cut from it.
