## Lucius – Legionär (Video 1, Ich-Figur)
Lucius, a Roman legionary of the 19th Legion in 9 AD, about 25 years old. Lean, weathered, tired. Short dark hair, stubble, dark brown eyes, a small scar on his chin. Carries a long javelin (pilum), a curved rectangular shield and a wooden pole with his pack, cooking pot and tools over his shoulder. Always soaked by rain.

## Varus – Feldherr (Video 1)
Publius Quinctilius Varus, Roman general and governor in 9 AD, a man in his fifties. Upright, self-assured posture. Ornate muscle cuirass, red general's cloak (paludamentum), helmet with a transverse crest, a short sword at his side. Clean, well-kept, out of place in the muddy forest.
- Persönlichkeit: selbstsicher, vertrauensselig gegenüber Arminius, eher Verwalter als Kämpfer.

## Arminius – Verräter (Video 1)
Arminius, a Germanic nobleman of the Cherusci in Roman service, around 25 years old. Tall, athletic, long light-brown hair tied back, short beard, sharp attentive eyes. Wears a mix of Roman officer's equipment and a heavy dark wool cloak with a bronze brooch. Calm, friendly smile toward the Romans; cold, determined look when alone.
- Persönlichkeit: charmant, klug, geduldig, doppeltes Spiel.

## Marcus – Bäcker (Video 2, Ich-Figur)
Marcus, a baker in Pompeii in 79 AD, in his forties. Stocky, strong forearms, short dark hair with grey at the temples, beard stubble, warm brown eyes, flour on his hands and face. Simple beige wool tunic, flour-dusted leather apron, sandals. Usually holds a long wooden bread peel. Works at a brick dome oven next to lava-stone millstones; round loaves scored into eight segments.
- Persönlichkeit: bodenständig, fleißig, stolz auf sein Brot, gelassen bei Beben, hängt an seiner Bäckerei.
- Stimme: warm, tief, ruhig – gegen Ende stockend.
- Eigenheiten: klopft Mehl von den Händen, prüft Brote mit dem Daumen, schaut zum Vesuv.

Albert, a stoker (fireman) on the RMS Titanic in April 1912, about 24 years old, from Southampton. Muscular, lean, soot-covered face and arms, sweat streaks through the coal dust, short hair under a dark flat cap, tired light eyes. Sleeveless grey undershirt, dark work trousers with braces, heavy boots, a rag around his neck. Always holds a coal shovel. Works in front of huge riveted boilers with glowing furnace doors.
