## Video 1 – Teutoburger Wald (9 n. Chr.)
```
Realistic cinematic historical short film, vertical 9:16, about 40 seconds, no narration, no on-screen text, no gore.
Germania, 9 AD. Main character: a weary young Roman legionary of the 19th Legion, short dark hair, stubble, iron Imperial helmet with cheek guards, segmented lorica armor over a faded red tunic, a wooden pole with his pack over his shoulder, a long javelin. Keep this exact character in every shot.
Shot 1: close-up side profile of the legionary in heavy rain, torchlight from the left, dark forest behind him.
Shot 2: a long column of Roman soldiers marching on a narrow muddy path through a dense misty pine forest, rain, cold green-grey light.
Shot 3: detail of his back: pack, shield, entrenching tools, rain running down the armor.
Shot 4: extreme close-up of his wet helmet, raindrops running down the iron.
Shot 5: high wide shot of the endless column winding between forested hills in fog.
Shot 6: at night by a tent and a torch, the Roman general Varus (crested helmet, red cloak) talks calmly with Arminius, a Germanic officer in Roman service (long hair, cloak).
Shot 7: the forest suddenly falls completely silent, rain stops, fog hangs motionless between the trees, the soldiers look around nervously.
Shot 8: spears fly out of the thicket from both sides toward the column, soldiers raise their shields, camera shakes, fast motion.
Shot 9: on a hill above, Arminius stands among Germanic warriors with torches, looking down at the trapped column.
Style: photorealistic, natural skin, 35mm film look, film grain, moody desaturated green-grey with warm orange torchlight, slow camera moves, accelerating pace at the ambush.
```

## Video 2 – Pompeji (79 n. Chr.)
```
Realistic cinematic historical short film, vertical 9:16, about 40 seconds, no narration, no on-screen text, no gore.
Pompeii, 79 AD. Main character: a Roman baker in his forties, short dark hair, beard stubble, simple beige tunic with a flour-dusted apron, strong forearms. Keep this exact character in every shot.
Shot 1: night, the baker in front of a glowing brick dome oven, sliding bread in with a long wooden peel, embers and smoke, lava-stone millstones in the background.
Shot 2: round Roman loaves divided into eight segments lie on a stone counter in warm oven light, dust in the air.
Shot 3: a sunny Pompeii street with stepping stones, merchants and amphorae; the ground trembles slightly, dust trickles from the walls, Mount Vesuvius at the end of the street.
Shot 4: wide shot over the rooftops: an enormous dark eruption column rises from Vesuvius and spreads at the top like a tree, the sky darkens.
Shot 5: fine grey ash begins to fall like snow over the street, then small pumice stones rain down, people cover their heads.
Shot 6: crowds run through the darkened streets toward the harbor, ash everywhere.
Shot 7: the baker stands in the doorway of his bakery, the oven still glowing behind him, ash falling in front of him, he hesitates.
Style: photorealistic, natural skin, 35mm film look, film grain, ochre and ash-grey palette with glowing red-orange fire, slow camera moves, rising tension.
```

```
Realistic cinematic historical short film, vertical 9:16, about 40 seconds, no narration, no on-screen text, no gore.
RMS Titanic, night of 14 April 1912. Main character: a ship's stoker in his twenties, soot-covered face, sweat, flat cap, sleeveless undershirt, work trousers, coal shovel. Keep this exact character in every shot.
Shot 1: a deep boiler room full of huge riveted boilers with glowing furnace doors; the stoker shovels coal into the fire, sparks and coal dust.
Shot 2: close-up of his sooty face in the orange firelight, sweat running down.
```
